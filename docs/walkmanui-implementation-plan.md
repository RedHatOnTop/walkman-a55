# WalkmanUI: architecture and status

2026-10-15. `android-ui/` is the NW-A55 stock music app (Sony `HgrmMediaPlayerApp`, Qt 5.3 QML)
running as an Android app on Qt 5.15.2. It supersedes the Codex plan this file used to hold
(phases 1-3); the brief that started it is `docs/walkmanui-full-app-brief.md`.

Two rules from the user shape everything:

- Port the Sony QML as it is. Do not invent UI. The Sony views are only fed and driven.
- Android's own navigation bar stays, so the stock bottom navigation bar moves: its Now Playing,
  Library and Option buttons go into the header, and Back is Android's Back.

Screen space is the constraint that decides placement. The device stage is 480x728 (the 800 px
panel at density 160, less Android's 24 px status bar and 48 px navigation bar), against the
stock 480x800 with its own 40 px bar. The header is the A55's: one info row with the playback
state and the volume (the WM1 series' 54 px volume area with its full-width bar and sound
quality text is not used), and Now Playing, Library and Option sit at its right end, where the
A55 shows its battery and Android's status bar now does. The row is 40 px, the stock bar's
height, so screens start 40 px down, against the A55's 28 px info row plus the 40 px bar at
the bottom. Screens that still come up short shrink a picture or a list (patch 0002) rather
than move anything under the player controls.

The probed screen tables (`work/device/probe`) come from a unit running Walkman One, which
reports itself as an NW-WM1Z, so they carry the WM1 series' configuration. The A55's own layout
comes from the NW-A50 Help Guide (helpguide.sony.net/dmp/nwa50) and the stock QML's other
branches, such as `useVolumeArea: false`.

## How it fits together

- **Resources.** `stage.py` copies the device QML tree (`work/qrc-device`, never committed), applies
  Qt 5.15 compatibility edits, applies `patches/*.patch`, redraws the two empty-list guide
  pictures for the top row (Pillow), adds `qml/`, and packs `build/res/stock.rcc` next to the
  fonts, translations and the two screen tables. `--android` also fills `build/android-pkg`.
- **Patches** (unified diffs against `work/qrc-device`, generated by scripts kept in
  `work/wui-takeover/patchgen/`):
  - 0001: in every window the navigation bar's buttons sit at the right end of the header's
    info row (the status icon row, 40 px tall); a tap left of them opens the volume popup, a
    tap on a disabled one does nothing; the option menu and dialogs follow.
  - 0002: on a screen shorter than the stock one: the library top drops its empty function
    icon row; the four player screens shrink their 408 px picture block; the DC phase
    linearizer and vinyl processor pictures shrink to 0.8 so the type pull-down clears the
    player controls; the language list leaves room for Cancel/OK; the Saved Sound Settings
    list keeps all three slots above its button.
  - 0003: the library help guide's picture is cut into bands that point at the moved Option
    button and at a real icon.
  - 0004: the text entry and volume popups start under the header.
  - 0005: the details and lyrics popup frames stretch (BorderImage), so the ViewModel can
    keep the stock 16 px gap under the shorter header and end them above the player controls.
  - 0006: Settings drops what Android owns or the A55 lacks and has no flag for: the empty
    quick bar (brightness is off by flag), Screen Off Timer, USB Connection Settings, Clock
    Settings, DSD Playback Settings, Format System Storage and Restore to Factory
    Configuration.
- **Frame.** `qml/AppFrame.qml` hosts windows, screens and popups in Loaders and animates
  transitions. `src/screencontroller.*` stands in for Sony's `ScreenTransitionController`: it
  reads `ScreenSettings.json`, keeps history and the four swipe neighbours, and begins every
  transition. A finger-driven change always slides and follows the finger
  (`src/swipegesture.*`, W.music ViewPager thresholds). Button-driven changes slide when they
  have a direction (Library and Now Playing move the player vertically, as W.music's pager does)
  and cross-fade otherwise. A press on a list, slider or other Flickable that can still move
  that way keeps the swipe from starting. Once the first screen is up, the stock `earlyload`
  views this build reaches (players, play queue, bookmarks, the sound pages from the player)
  are built in the background one at a time, so a swipe never waits for its page.
- **ViewModels.** One `ScreenModel` per Sony view (`src/models/`) supplies its root properties
  and handles its signals by name, as the stock C++ ViewModels did. `src/appcontext.*` creates
  them and owns the shared flows: context menus, list dialogs, yes/no questions, text entry
  (Android's keyboard through a stand-in for `ext.lib.inputcontextproxy`), bookmarks, playlists,
  details and first-display help guides. Swipes between the sound pages follow the WM1 series'
  set this build keeps (equalizer or tone control, DSEE HX, DC phase linearizer, dynamic
  normalizer, vinyl processor, wrapping).
- **Settings and sound.** `src/models/settingsmodels.*` serve every settings screen (values in
  prefs under `settings/`; language applies live and at startup); `src/models/soundmodels.*`
  serve the sound pages in their player, settings and DAC variants, sharing
  `src/models/soundstate.*` (prefs under `sound/`, three saved slots). Screens of hardware the A55 lacks
  (Bluetooth, NC/ASM, remote, USB DAC) get defaults so they render. Settings lists only what
  this app can do on Android (user, 2026-10-15): flags and patch 0006 take out brightness,
  the screen timeout, USB connection, auto power off, the clock, the keyboard choice, high
  gain output, DSD playback settings, storage format and factory reset, and L/R balance
  (user, 2026-10-16: nobody uses it); their screens stay in
  the table unlinked. The WM1 series' ten-band equalizer and tone control stay on purpose
  (user: limiting the A55 to fewer bands was software discrimination).
- **Sound effects.** On the NW-A55 image (user, 2026-10-16: only that hardware) the system
  effect library `android-ui/audiofx/libwalkmanfx.so` runs on every music session through
  `audio_effects.conf`'s `output_session_processing`; `src/soundeffects.cpp` sends it the
  SoundState (plus whether the track is hi-res) through `SoundEffects.java`, an AudioEffect on
  the output mix that is never enabled. Our own DSEE HX, equalizer or tone control, DC phase
  linearizer, vinyl processor, dynamic normalizer and ClearAudio+ (Sony's are unpublished);
  Source Direct is bit exact. Elsewhere (Waydroid) the effect is absent and the settings are
  only kept. Details: `devlog/2026-10-16-walkman-sound-effects.md`.
- **Lyrics.** `library/lyrics.*` reads a `.lrc` beside the song or the lyrics tag (LRC or plain
  text; UTF-8, UTF-16, CP949). The lyrics popup follows the position, centres the playing
  line, seeks to a tapped line, and leaves the list where the user scrolled it for 5 s.
- **Library.** `library/musicdb.*` scans the storages on a thread into a cached track table
  (tags from `library/tagreader.*`, which reads FLAC, MP3, MP4/ALAC, Ogg Vorbis/Opus, WAV and
  AIFF without decoding), groups it for every browse list, and keeps playlists (`.m3u`/`.m3u8`
  on storage, read-only; the app's own in its data directory, editable).
- **Playback.** `playback/player.*` plays a queue of library tracks with QMediaPlayer: the stock
  0-120 volume scale, AVLS (the volume capped at 58, the stock app's AVLS maximum in the probe),
  repeat and shuffle, seek, scan while held, and a persisted queue that resumes paused. Playback
  Range "All Range" goes on from the end of a list into the next list of the level it was picked
  from (the next album of the album list, the next album of that artist, the next playlist;
  not after an "All" row or a folder), and repeat all wraps that range; "Selected Range" stops
  with the list. The range is not kept across a restart or a library rescan. Artist lists
  group by album artist or song artist as Artist List Display says. Keep Screen On holds the
  display on while the lyrics popup is open (`keepScreenOn` in `playback/platform.*`). `playback/platform.*` and `android/src/org/walkmanui/PlaybackService.java`
  add the media session, notification, audio focus and becoming-noisy handling on Android.

## Building

    android-ui/build.sh host                   # build/host/walkmanui
    android-ui/build.sh android                # one APK, armeabi-v7a + x86_64
    android-ui/build.sh android armeabi-v7a    # the device image's APK (no x86_64 libs)

Host checks run under Xvfb with the tools in `work/wui-takeover/` (`run.sh` takes a
`WALKMANUI_SCRIPT` of steps: tap, drag, press/move/release, key:back, go, expect, grab, dump,
frames). On Android a launch carries no environment, so a script written to
`<external files dir>/walkmanui-script.txt` runs once and its output goes to logcat.

## Verified

- Host (Xvfb, software renderer): all 111 non-blank screens load; library, browse lists,
  playlists, hi-res, folders, play queue, bookmarks, context menus, details, volume, help guides,
  icon select, icon drag-and-drop, playlist create/rename/delete, swipes on every pager,
  every settings and sound screen with its values, the sound pages' swipe cycle both ways,
  Saved Sound Settings save/read with the stock dialogs, lyrics (timed .lrc, LRC and plain
  tags, unreadable file, none), language kept across a restart.
- At the device's stage height (`WALKMANUI_HEIGHT=728`: 480x800 panel at density 160 minus
  Android's bars) all 111 screens load, and the player, library, play queue, bookmarks,
  language list and every sound page from the player fit without anything under the player
  controls; on Waydroid's shorter window (480x674) the sound pages fit too. The header buttons
  open the player, the library and the option menu, a disabled one ignores a tap, and a tap
  elsewhere in the header opens the volume popup. The info row shows play or pause while a
  track is loaded and the volume, at the A55's places.
- Settings on the host with two 2 s albums: All Range plays from the end of one album into the
  next, stops on the range's first track at its end, and with repeat all wraps to the first
  album; Selected Range stops on the album's first track; Artist List Display regroups the
  artist list (album artist "Duo Band" against song artists Ann and Bob); AVLS brings 84 down
  to 58, holds + at 58, shows the AVLS mark in the header and the popup, and lets go when off.
- Waydroid (Android 13, x86_64, `192.168.240.112:5555`): the APK installs, asks for storage,
  scans shared storage, plays (position advances, media session reaches PLAYING), Back works.
  Frame times with the window drawing continuously (`frames:` in the test driver): player to
  sound page and five sound swipes 16.7 ms median, 3 frames over 25 ms (worst 37 ms, first run
  after install); later swipe runs and a lyrics drag 0 frames over 25 ms (worst 17.6 ms).
- `library/tests/tst_tagreader` against ffprobe: 49 generated fixtures plus damaged copies.
  `library/tests/tst_lyrics`: 21 parser, lookup and decoding checks.
  `make -C android-ui/audiofx test`: the effect processing on synthetic signals, and the built
  library loaded and driven the way the effects factory, AudioFlinger and the app do.

## Not verified

- The NW-A55 itself (armeabi-v7a, API 22): nothing in this build has run on it.
  `PlaybackService` on API 22, frame times on the device GPU and audible output (the audio HAL is
  a separate track) are open.
- Nobody has heard the sound effects: whether the image's AudioPolicyEffects attaches them,
  mediaserver loading the library under the device's SELinux mode, the hidden AudioEffect API
  by reflection on API 22 and the Cortex-A7's CPU load are unchecked. The output settings are
  still kept, not applied.
- Keep Screen On has not run on Android (the host has no such flag). The player's ClearAudio+/Source Direct toggle stays hidden; the A55 keeps
  ClearAudio+ in Settings. BtAutoConnectSetting's title is the untranslated "230006" in
  Sony's files.
- The software renderer leaves a stale layer on a dropped library icon; the OpenGL backend and
  Android do not. Use `BACKEND=` (the default backend) when that matters.

## Device deployment (operator)

The device image embeds the APK as a system app (`tools/cm_customize.py` pre-extracts its
armeabi-v7a libraries). The package is now `org.walkmanui`; the earlier builds were
`org.qtproject.example.walkmanui`.

    android-ui/build.sh android armeabi-v7a
    cp android-ui/build/android/WalkmanUI.apk work/android/aosp-ford/apps/WalkmanUI.apk
    # then rebuild and flash the ford system image through the image session's usual steps
    # the sound effects: the library and both audio_effects.conf (build.sh android builds it)
    python3 -I android-ui/audiofx/embed.py work/android/aosp-ford/system.img
